## Same country. Different measures.

What if we keep the same 1,000 blocks, but change what each block represents?

Land area gives one Germany. Population, GDP, tree cover, grassland, and built-up land give five others.

For me, the interesting part is not the distortion itself, but the questions it creates: **Where do people, economic activity, and environmental resources align — and where do they not?**

The same framework could be extended to heat exposure, air pollution, energy, infrastructure, climate risk, or other dimensions of human–environment interactions.

A map does not always have to provide the answer. Sometimes its value is making a better research question visible.

### About the maps

Each map contains 1,000 equal-sized blocks, but the quantity represented by a block changes between maps. The cartograms redistribute the blocks according to population, GDP, or land-cover area while preserving the regional structure of Germany as far as possible.

Land-cover estimates are derived from ESA WorldCover 2021. The cartograms were created using the open-source `popgrid` framework.
